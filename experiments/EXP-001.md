# EXP-001: Cross-tissue reproducibility of aging-associated signals

**Status:** preregistered-style research plan, not yet run. **Type:** computational and read-only public data.

## Primary question
Do some age-associated molecular pathways show reproducible direction, independently of tissue and cohort, after accounting for cell composition and technical batch effects?

## Hypotheses
- H1: A subset of pathway-level associations with chronological age will generalize across held-out human cohorts and tissues.
- H0: Apparent cross-tissue consistency disappears under independent validation, batch adjustment, and cell-composition controls.

## Data acquisition gate (metadata prototype implemented; approval pending)
Five **candidate** public sources are listed in [the source registry](../data/source_registry.json). The deterministic readiness gate lives in [`phiage/sources.py`](../phiage/sources.py). Run `python -m phiage sources data/source_registry.json` to produce an auditable source receipt and rejection reasons.

Every entry is currently **not approved for analysis**. Discovery metadata and website accessibility do not establish reuse permission, sample-to-donor mapping, independence, availability of a suitable processed expression matrix, or cohort age covariates.

Before promotion of any source: identify properly licensed, deidentified public human transcriptomic age-series; validate accession, tissue, platform, donor unit, available age resolution, age distribution, processing, access/permission, sample overlap, and independent holdout eligibility. Record checksums for actually curated data. No restricted age metadata or raw personal genomes may be fetched by the open prototype.

### Specific confounder alerts
- **GTEx**: V11 is multi-tissue but age is public in decades, exact donor age protected. Multiple tissue samples from the same donor must be grouped together; releases V8/V10/V11 are NOT independent cohorts.
- **Fibroblast datasets**: culture and cell passage may dominate organismal-age patterns; expression measured from fibroblasts is not skin function.
- **Single cell**: cells from the same donor are pseudoreplicates, not separate human samples.
- **Blood**: cell composition, infection and other immune covariates require explicit sensitivity checks.

No statistical hypothesis test has yet been executed on human molecular data.

## Live provenance probe (v0.3; structural only)
`python -m phiage geo-probe GSE307377 --allow-network --with-counts` inspects the GEO Series Matrix sample accession metadata and the published `GSE307377_raw.txt.gz` supplementary table header. It logs source URLs and SHA-256 checksums, and **always** records `analysis_ready: false`.

This does **not** establish independence of the nine donors, correctness of age labels, normalization suitability, reuse permissions, or a biological association. No gene-level hypothesis testing runs in this stage. The pull request includes a non-blocking live source probe; external outages cannot transform candidate studies into findings.

## Methods to implement
1. Build a frozen manifest of cohorts and explicit training/held-out split.
2. Normalize within a dataset using predeclared, modality-appropriate procedures.
3. Fit age-associated pathway effects within each cohort, adjusting for documented covariates when possible.
4. Perform random-effects meta-analysis only when comparability assumptions hold; show heterogeneity and uncertainty.
5. Replicate in holdouts, including sensitivity tests for cell composition, batch, missingness and publication bias.
6. Produce signed/hashed evidence receipts and skeptic-agent critiques of each claim.
7. Do not interpret association as causality. Reserve causal language for justified perturbations or suitable causal designs.

## Primary measures
- Directional agreement across independent cohorts
- Held-out effect uncertainty and false discovery rate
- Cross-tissue heterogeneity and sensitivity to documented confounders
- Replication failure rate

## Stop / reject conditions
- Identity leakage or overlapping cohorts disguised as independent
- Missing permissions or insufficient provenance
- Uncontrolled confounding that invalidates interpretation
- Models outperform baselines only on training cohorts

## Success criterion
At least one pathway survives predeclared replication and sensitivity thresholds on held-out data, with a reproducible artifact. **This would identify a candidate association, not prove reversal of aging.**

## Safety boundary
No participant recruitment, wet-lab protocol, compound dosing, clinical advice, or unapproved human experimentation is part of this phase.

## v0.4: Library-label crosswalk and data QC

Primary GEO records expose per-sample `Description: Library name`. A nine-row crosswalk at [data/crosswalks/GSE307377_gsm_to_library.json](../data/crosswalks/GSE307377_gsm_to_library.json) links GSM identifiers and public age groups to raw and TPM column names. `python -m phiage crosswalk` rejects missing/duplicated/misassigned labels, wrong age characteristics and mismatched table suffixes. Original archived source receipts stay immutable.

`python -m phiage expression-qc --allow-network` performs *aggregate technical QC* of the two hash-pinned public files and explicitly reports missing/nonfinite cells, table dimensions, duplicate feature IDs and column totals. It does **not** test aging hypotheses or analyze gene effects. The table's `Transcript/RepeatID` identifier semantics, independent-donor review, covariates, reuse rights and multi-cohort validation remain open.

## Observed numeric gate from v0.4

The deposited raw table contains **73,187 non-integer cells** among 27,142 features × nine samples. This disallows silently applying methods that assume unmodified integer sequencing counts. Both tables have nine columns, no missing/nonfinite/negative cells and no duplicate feature identifiers. Feature-ID semantics and independent donor mapping remain unverified; biological analysis has **not** run.


## v0.5 preregistered technical sub-study: feature semantics and sample QC

The source dataset is included in a *published* multi-model paper, [Nucleosome stability safeguards cell identity, stress resilience and healthy aging](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12458469/). Reanalysis of its GEO data is not independent replication.

The next phase quantifies syntactic classes of `Transcript/RepeatID` entries, literal annotation tags, and technical log1p(TPM) sample similarity. It explicitly avoids fold changes, p-values, clinical aging measures, or biological claims. Identity and processing semantics require curator review before selecting features/pathways, and a separate donor-level independent cohort is required before confirmatory work.

`python -m phiage feature-qc --allow-network` records a pinned-GEO technical receipt. Its status always remains `analysis_ready:false` and `claim_level:TECHNICAL_QC_ONLY`.

## Candidate independent lifespan cohort (GSE113957, metadata only)

Fleischer et al. (2018), [Predicting age from the transcriptome of human dermal fibroblasts](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6300908/), published a larger human fibroblast transcriptomic cohort: 133 healthy people aged 1–94, plus 10 people with Hutchinson-Gilford progeria syndrome. We have added [GSE113957](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE113957) as a sixth *candidate* source, **not** an approved replication dataset.

Gate for use: verify exact sample-level cohort membership and age labels, prove non-overlap of donors, determine access/licensing terms and independent quality, harmonize relevant RefSeq feature IDs and transcript/gene reference mappings, and handle source-platform/FPKM-vs-TPM differences without erasing study-specific effects. Exclude HGPS samples from ordinary healthy chronological-age comparisons unless a separate preregistered analysis specifies otherwise. Reanalysis papers based on GSE113957 are not new independent datasets.

## v0.6 metadata-only candidate source census

GSE113957 is a candidate larger fibroblast cohort from a [published 2018 study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6300908/), **not independent validation yet**. A read-only `cohort-probe` subcommand fetches official public GEO Series Matrix metadata only, checks GSM uniqueness, conservatively parses donor age characteristics when unambiguous, and counts only explicit sample-level healthy/HGPS labels. Labels that are unknown or contradictory stay unknown. It records a source byte checksum and human-readable rejection gates, but never downloads protected medical data, analyzes expression or classifies disease from name-coded sample IDs.

No differential expression or age prediction is authorized. A minimum follow-on review must establish donor uniqueness, precise healthy/HGPS cohort mapping, access/reuse terms, source processed FPKM file and column linkage, and harmonization of RefSeq features and sample covariates. Source observations must not be confused with reproducible aging biology.
