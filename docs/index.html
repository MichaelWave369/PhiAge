The rules of the lab

Correlations ≠ causes. Each proposed mechanism needs an explicit falsifier.

Markers ≠ outcomes. Epigenetic clocks do not substitute for human healthspan or lifespan.

Independent replication matters. Two papers describing one cohort are not two discoveries.

Safety is part of the science. Repair, cancer suppression, immunity and tradeoffs must be evaluated together.
